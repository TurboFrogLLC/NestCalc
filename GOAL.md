### Frozen Residual Scope

#### A - Wire Exact New Chrome From The Residual Package

1. **A1 AutoNest result card.** Match the residual card, dense pack, factual
   blue 0-degree and orange 90-degree presentation, and connected meta segment:
   Orientation dark; Trim frost. Do not change AutoNest facts or algorithms.
2. **A2 Footer counts.** Manual shows TOTAL and MANUAL only. AutoNest shows
   TOTAL, MANUAL, and AUTO.
3. **A3 SOURCE header.** Preserve the exact left-to-right order: Source,
   Lucide File, Lucide Eraser, IN|MM, Chevron. Do not substitute text buttons
   or non-authority icons.
4. **A4 Custom dialogs.** Match the accepted frost Save preset, Edit quick
   value, and Rename dialogs with dark inputs and Lucide Check, X, and Trash.
   Do not use browser prompt/confirm chrome or invent dialog controls.
5. **A5 Keypad to Calculator.** Numbers remain fixed. Orange operations slide
   right in `+`, minus, multiply, divide order. The 50px LCD occupies the fixed
   68px mid-slot. Overall height is constant and only width expands. Preserve
   the authority toolbar, quick-value strip, and full-width Enter behavior.

#### B - Revert Or Restore Exact V2 Behavior

1. **B1 Base keypad and Calculator look.** Restore the V2 numpad base look and
   press lift wherever the residual package does not explicitly override it.
2. **B2 PR #60 regressions.** Repair only a regression demonstrated against a
   concrete V2 behavior plus prior green proof and belonging to A1-A5, B1-B4,
   or C1-C4. B2 is not open-ended cleanup. If the evidence is not concrete or
   the fix requires a non-Allowed File, stop for a human decision.

   not summon or operate the keypad; those fields are inert until reopened.
4. **B4 SOURCE motion.** SOURCE expand/collapse is right-anchored, does not
   blink, and does not fly in from left to right. The SOURCE control already
   exists in V2; this human-supplied behavior outcome authorizes no invented
   timing, easing, geometry, or extra chrome. If the outcome cannot be reached
   with exact authority styling through the bridge, stop.

#### C - Bridge Or Behavior Only

1. **C1 Unit consistency.** SOURCE IN|MM owns the unit interpretation of
   textarea numbers. Bounds and PART SIZE follow the active SOURCE unit and
   convert consistently when toggled. Do not change parser or engine math.
2. **C2 Numeric sanitization.** Across the hosted HowMany product surface,
   numeric entry accepts digits plus at most one decimal point and rejects
   letters. Achieve this through the allowed host/bridge seam only.
3. **C3 Presets and quick values.** Preset names accept multiple characters.
   Quick-value chips set the focused value instead of appending. Selection
   feedback is blink only. Preserve owner-scoped preset storage semantics.
4. **C4 MARGINS badge.** The collapsed MARGINS badge always reflects the
   current expanded left, right, bottom, and top field values.
